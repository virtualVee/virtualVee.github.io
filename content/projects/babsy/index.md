## The idea

Babsy explores how everyday baby routines can be easier to record and review. The focus is on quick interactions and readable records, so logging fits around the routine itself.

## What I’ve worked on

- **Core recording:** Building feeding timers, saved feeding records and options to edit or delete entries, alongside an initial nappy-recording screen.
- **Simpler interactions:** Moving from separate screens towards clearer action groups, and prototyping a daily timeline to put events in context.
- **Data considerations:** Exploring local data ownership and export, with an emphasis on giving users control over their records.

## Making recording easier

I explored grouping frequent actions and showing the day’s events together, aiming to reduce navigation. The timeline is still a prototype; the next step is to test whether this approach makes recording easier.

## Working with AI

AI-assisted design discussions and prototyping help me explore alternatives, including the timeline layout and options for data ownership. These explorations inform the project; they do not establish that a design is usable or that a feature is ready for release.

## Progress and next steps

The app is still in development. Feeding records use local storage; nappy records are currently held only in screen state. The timeline remains a separate prototype, and export has not yet been implemented.

Next, I’m focusing on reliable recording and persistence, then testing whether the simpler layout makes everyday tasks easier. Usability and wider use by parents still need validation.
