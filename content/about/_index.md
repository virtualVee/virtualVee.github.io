I work in healthcare technology, where understanding a problem often means looking at both how a system works and how people use it.

My background spans technical assurance, process improvement, documentation and software testing. I enjoy bringing people, processes and technical teams together: asking questions, making sense of complex information and turning it into clear, practical next steps.

I’m naturally curious and like getting into the detail of how things work. Whether I’m analysing a process, testing a change or documenting a system, I want to make things easier to understand and use.

This portfolio brings together projects that reflect that approach, along with what I’m learning as I go.
